Welcome

The Seven Noahide Laws — known in Hebrew as the Sheva Mitzvot B'nei Noach — are a set of universal moral principles that, according to Jewish tradition, apply to all of humanity. Rooted in ancient Scripture and rabbinic teaching, these laws form the ethical foundation for a just and compassionate world.

This site is a resource for those seeking to understand, study, and live by these timeless principles — regardless of background or tradition.

The Seven Fundamental Laws

These seven laws constitute the basic moral code binding upon all humanity:

  1. Belief in One G-d — Do not worship idols or false deities.
  2. Respect G-d — Do not blaspheme the name of God.
  3. Respect Human Life — Do not take an innocent human life.
  4. Respect Property — Do not steal or deprive others of their rightful property.
  5. Good Family Relationship — Do not engage in forbidden sexual relations.
  6. Respect All Creatures — Do not eat flesh torn from a living animal.
  7. Establishment Honest and Moral Govenment and Justice System — Uphold law, order, and justice in society.
